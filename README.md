# Tidebreak (working title)

A mobile-first 5v5 MOBA concept, built in Unity, designed to compete with the top of the genre on the things mobile players actually feel: match length, fairness, social health, and device reach. Its signature: a tide that rises on a known schedule, with a river current whose direction is revealed only at the moment it turns.

- **[Game concept](docs/game-concept.md)**: pitch, pillars, map, the tide and the Turn, positions and the jungle, heroes, systems, monetization, tech, launch plan, and risks.
- **[Art direction & visual development plan](docs/art-direction.md)**: the look, readability rules, the water, characters, Unity rendering setup, and the visual work completed before production code.

Status: concept / pre-production.
